The SLIX philosophy

Every drop has a purpose.

A sprinkler system is only as good as its design and its upkeep. Heads placed with care, zones matched to the ground they water, and a system checked through the seasons. That's the difference between a lawn that's patchy and one that's even, edge to edge.

  • CoverageConsistent watering across the whole lawn, without dry corners.
  • EfficiencyWater goes to the grass and beds, not the driveway.
  • ReliabilityA system that turns on when it should and does its job.
  • The resultOutdoor spaces that look cared for all season.

Built for Michigan

Rooted in Tawas City. Ready for Michigan.

SLIX is based in Tawas City, on the shore of Tawas Bay. Up here, a sprinkler system lives through two very different jobs: keeping lawns green through a short, warm summer, and surviving a winter that freezes the ground solid.

Water left in the lines when the frost comes can crack pipes, fittings, and heads. That's why seasonal care matters here: a proper startup in spring, and a thorough shutdown before the hard freeze.
